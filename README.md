# Retail Analytics in R

## Overview
This project demonstrates an end-to-end **retail analytics workflow in R**, using a realistic, warehouse-style dataset. It mirrors real-world business problems faced by retail analytics, finance, and operations teams, and focuses on translating data into **actionable business insights**.

The dataset is the same one used in my companion projects:
- Retail Analytics in PostgreSQL (data modeling & SQL analytics)
- Retail Analytic in Power BI (dashboarding & visualization)

This repository focuses on **advanced analytics and business reasoning using R**.

---

## Business Objectives
The project answers key retail questions such as:
- How are revenue and profit evolving over time?
- Which products and categories truly drive profitability?
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Do promotions increase profit or erode margins?
- How can demand be forecasted to support inventory planning?
- Where do operational inefficiencies impact performance?

---

## Dataset
The dataset simulates a real retail data warehouse and includes:
- **Fact tables**: orders, shippings, returns  
- **Dimension tables**: products, customers, retailers, warehouses, promotions, calendar  

It spans multiple regions, channels, product categories, and years (2021–2024).

## Key Analyses
- **Data Validation & Feature Engineering**
  - Revenue, profit, margin, markup
  - Return handling and logistics cost integration
- **Exploratory Analysis**
  - Revenue & profit trends
  - Category, region, and channel performance
- **Customer Analytics**
  - RFM segmentation
  - Revenue concentration (Pareto analysis)
- **Product Profitability**
  - Revenue vs profit trade-offs
  - Loss leader identification
- **Promotions & Pricing**
  - Discount impact on margin and profit
  - Promotion effectiveness analysis
- **Demand Forecasting**
  - Monthly product demand forecasting using ETS
- **Executive Recommendations**
  - Clear, decision-oriented insights for leadership
